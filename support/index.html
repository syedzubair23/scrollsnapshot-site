Support

Questions, bug reports and help with ScrollSnapshot.

Email the developer

For questions, bug reports, or help with ScrollSnapshot, email zubairdev27@gmail.com.

Reporting a bug

These help the most:

  • Your Chrome version (from chrome://version).
  • The capture mode you used, and the engine if you turned Turbo on.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.

Only send a screenshot or a page address if you are comfortable sharing it.

Ideas and feedback

Something you'd like it to do, or something that got in your way? Send feedback. It takes a minute, and an email address is optional.
