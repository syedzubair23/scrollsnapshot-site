Cut a capture into slices later

You do not have to decide to slice before you capture. Any result can be cut afterwards, into rows or columns, and handed over as the same numbered ZIP that Slices mode makes.

IN THIS GUIDEOpen the slicerPlace the cutsAcross or downA ZIP like any otherAll guides ↗
01

Open the slicer

On a finished capture, choose Slice from the result toolbar. The capture stays in view, and the bar changes to the slicing controls. Nothing is cut until you press the button that makes the ZIP.

02

Place the cuts

Click the image to make a cut at that point. Drag a cut to move it. Use a ruler to place one at an exact position, cut every set number of pixels, or cut into a set number of equal parts. You can also keep cuts out of lines of text, so a sentence is not split across two images.

  • Equal parts suit a long page you want to share as a set. Every N pixels suits a fixed-height format, such as a slide.
  • Turn on the option to keep cuts out of lines of text for pages with dense copy.
03

Across or down

The slice cutter cuts down as well as across through Cut direction. Rows suit a tall capture. Columns suit a wide one, such as a spreadsheet or a timeline.

04

A ZIP like any other

The result is a slices ZIP, the same numbered archive Slices mode makes, with a manifest of where each piece belongs. Download it, or use Reassemble, which is on the toolbar of every slices capture, to build one image again. Reassemble puts columns back side by side as readily as it stacks rows.

  1. Open a finished capture and choose Slice.
  2. Choose a direction, then click, drag, or use a ruler to set the cuts.
  3. Download the ZIP, or reassemble the pieces into one image.
KEEP IN MIND

Cutting a capture later works on the pixels you already have. For a page too tall for one image, Slices mode still keeps full resolution from the start.
