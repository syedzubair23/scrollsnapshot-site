A screenshot that fits the document

PDF export is for a capture that needs to be filed, printed, or reviewed as a document. Choose one tall page, or let ScrollSnapshot divide the image across A4 or Letter pages.

IN THIS GUIDEPaper is a different boundaryExport from the finished imageAll guides ↗
01

Paper is a different boundary

Tall keeps the captured page as one PDF page. A4 and Letter add paper-sized pages with margins. For supported captures, ScrollSnapshot uses line positions from the page to place breaks between visible lines where it can, rather than cutting through a row of text.

02

Export from the finished image

  1. Capture and make any crop or edits first.
  2. Choose PDF in the result page, then Tall, A4, or Letter in the Page control.
  3. Download and review the page breaks before sharing or printing.
KEEP IN MIND

PDF is made from the captured pixels. It is an image document, not a reflowed copy of the web page's text.
