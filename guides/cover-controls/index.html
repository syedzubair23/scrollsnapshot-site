View, copy or remove one cover

Point at a cover and three small buttons appear beside it: View, Copy and Remove. Together with peek and Hide tab title, they let you work with a covered page without taking the covers off.

IN THIS GUIDEA cover's buttonsPeek under them allHide tab titleAll guides ↗
01

A cover's buttons

  1. View shows what is under that one cover while you hold it down, and covers it again the moment you let go.
  2. Copy puts the hidden text on your clipboard without showing it on screen. An area cover has no Copy, since pictures and video have no text.
  3. Remove takes the cover off; for a kept cover, its rule goes too.
02

Peek under them all

Hold Ctrl+Alt, or Control+Option on a Mac, to see through every cover on the page while the keys are down.

03

Hide tab title

On the toolbar, Hide tab title keeps the tab strip from showing the page's title, which often carries a name or an account. Captures taken while it is hidden are named after the site instead.

KEEP IN MIND

The buttons appear when you point at a cover; there is no keyboard path to them. They, the Copied note and anything you are viewing are never part of a capture.
