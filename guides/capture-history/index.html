Find the capture you already made

Capture history keeps recent results on your device so a useful image does not vanish when its result tab closes. Browse thumbnails, search by page title, filter by mode, or mark a favorite.

IN THIS GUIDERetention is a choiceRetrieve or removeAll guides ↗
01

Retention is a choice

Settings controls how many captures to keep and whether older ones expire. Turning Capture history off prunes the store to the current result; the next capture replaces it. Batch requires history on so every successful page survives its queue.

02

Retrieve or remove

  1. Open History from the popup or Settings.
  2. Search for a title or filter by Visible, Full page, Slices, or Region.
  3. Open, download, favorite, or delete a capture. Clear history asks before removing everything.
KEEP IN MIND

Captures and their page details stay on this device. Some extension settings may use Chrome Sync between your own signed-in browsers; the images do not go to a ScrollSnapshot server.
