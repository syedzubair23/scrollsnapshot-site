Put your name on what you save

A watermark marks a capture as yours. Add text, a logo, or both in Settings, and ScrollSnapshot stamps them onto what you save.

IN THIS GUIDESet it up onceWhere it appearsSwitch it off for one captureTry itAll guides ↗
01

Set it up once

Open Settings, then Watermark. Add your text, a logo, or both. Place it in a corner, along an edge, in the centre, or tiled across the image, and set its size, opacity, text colour, and distance from the edge. A live preview shows the result as you change it.

It is off until you turn it on, and the logo stays on this device.

02

Where it appears

The watermark shows over the capture on the result page, so you see it before you save. It goes on Download, Copy, PDF, and slices.

03

Switch it off for one capture

Once you have set a watermark up, a stamp button appears on the result toolbar. Its tooltip says whether the watermark is on or off for this capture, so you can leave it off for one image without touching your settings.

04

Try it

  1. Open Settings and choose Watermark.
  2. Enter text or add a logo, then choose a position and opacity.
  3. Capture something, check the preview on the result page, and download it.
KEEP IN MIND

A watermark marks an image; it does not hide anything. Use Redact when private details must be covered.
