Change your mind after drawing

Marks stay editable until you apply them. A shape comes up selected with handles to resize it, and the Select tool picks up any mark when you want to adjust it later.

IN THIS GUIDEPick a mark upMove, nudge, and tidyAny colour you likeA reliable sequenceAll guides ↗
01

Pick a mark up

The Select tool (V) picks up any mark on the capture. The drawing tools draw over others, so you can lay a new mark on top of an old one without grabbing it by accident. A new shape comes up selected, with handles to resize it, a grip to rotate it, and a grip on each end of an arrow.

02

Move, nudge, and tidy

Drag to move a mark, or use the arrow keys to nudge it. Hold Shift while rotating to snap to 15-degree steps. Double-click text to rewrite it. Delete removes the mark, Ctrl+D duplicates it, and ] and [ move it forward or back in the stack.

Changing the colour, stroke, or text size while a mark is selected changes that mark, not just the next one you draw.

03

Any colour you like

The colour popover opens with twelve named colours as small circles. A sixth swatch opens a full picker: a colour field, a hue strip, a hex box, your recent colours, and an eyedropper (I) that takes a pixel straight off the capture, so an annotation can match the page it explains.

04

A reliable sequence

  1. Draw a shape, a line, or a note.
  2. Press V, then pick the mark up to move, resize, rotate, or restyle it.
  3. Use Ctrl+D and the arrow keys to repeat a mark at an exact distance, then apply the marks.
KEEP IN MIND

Marks preview over the capture until you apply them, so you can keep adjusting as long as you need.
