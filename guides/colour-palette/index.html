See the colours a page is made of

The palette lists the colours a capture is made of: the background, and the small colours that carry the design, like a button, a badge or the text. Each has its hex value and its share of the image.

IN THIS GUIDEOpen it from the iconWhy the small colours show upAll guides ↗
01

Open it from the icon

  1. Finish a capture and open its result page.
  2. Click the palette icon at the top right, under Done.
  3. Click a colour to copy its hex, or Copy all for the whole palette, one hex per line.
  4. Press Escape or click anywhere else to close it.
02

Why the small colours show up

Ranking colours by how many pixels they cover fills a palette with shades of the background, so ScrollSnapshot also weighs how different each colour is from those already listed. A blue button on a white page makes the list even though it covers a sliver of it, while a fourth shade of grey does not.

Shades the eye cannot tell apart count as one colour, and each colour is shown as a value that is really on the page, never a blend of its neighbours. Specks too small to be part of the design, and transparent pixels, are left out.

KEEP IN MIND

The palette summarises a capture. For the exact colour of one pixel, use the colour picker.
