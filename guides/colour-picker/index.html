Take the colour from the page

The colour picker is for the one pixel you actually mean. Point at the page, inspect the enlarged area and hex value, then click to copy it.

IN THIS GUIDEWhy it lives on the pageChoose a pixelAll guides ↗
01

Why it lives on the page

The extension popup closes when focus moves to the page, so the picker itself handles the copy. It samples the device pixel under the pointer instead of inventing an average colour from a high-density display.

02

Choose a pixel

  1. Start Pick a colour from the popup.
  2. Move the loupe over the pixel you need and read the displayed hex.
  3. Click to copy it, or press Escape to leave without choosing.
KEEP IN MIND

Use the colour palette on a finished capture when you want the dominant colours, rather than one exact pixel.
