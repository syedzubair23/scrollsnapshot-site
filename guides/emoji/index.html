Put an emoji on it

An emoji says “yes”, “look here” or “this is broken” faster than a label. The Emoji tool places one with a click, in the editor and in the region toolbar alike.

IN THIS GUIDEChoose and placeAny emoji at allChange it afterwardsAll guides ↗
01

Choose and place

  1. Open the editor and choose the Emoji tool, or press E. Its button shows the emoji the next click places.
  2. Open the menu beside it and pick one from the panel.
  3. Click the capture where it should go.
02

Any emoji at all

The panel starts with the ones screenshots are usually marked up with: check marks and crosses, warnings, pointing hands, a bug, a light bulb, faces. For anything else, paste it into the field under the grid, or type it with your system's emoji keyboard (Windows key + period on Windows, Control + Command + Space on a Mac), and press Use.

03

Change it afterwards

An emoji is a mark like any other: pick it up to move, resize or rotate it. With an emoji selected, choosing another one in the panel swaps it in place. An emoji keeps its own colours, so the colour button does not apply to it.
