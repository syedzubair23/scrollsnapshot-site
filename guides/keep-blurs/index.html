Keep blurs on a page or a site

A cover you place by hand lasts until the page reloads. Keep it, and it comes back on that page, or across the whole site, each time you open it.

IN THIS GUIDEKeep themHow they come backWhen one isn't foundRemove themAll guides ↗
01

Keep them

  1. Cover what you want with Element or Area.
  2. Open Keep blurs on the toolbar.
  3. Choose Keep on this page, or Keep on this site for every page of it.
02

How they come back

An element cover is kept as a rule that covers every element like it, so a list of account numbers stays covered however long it grows. An area cover comes back only when the window is the width it was drawn at, to within a pixel, since at any other width the page underneath has moved. Word covers are never kept.

03

When one isn't found

If a page has changed and a kept cover cannot find what it covered, ScrollSnapshot says so rather than staying quiet: the popup shows how many applied, such as 2 of 3 saved blurs applied. A capture you take still goes ahead, with a warning to check it; a batch skips that page, and an agent is refused.

04

Remove them

Remove a cover with its own Remove button and its rule is forgotten too. Settings → Live blur lists every site with kept blurs, each with Remove, and Remove all asks before it clears them.

KEEP IN MIND

Kept blurs, and the list of sites that have them, stay on this device and are never synced. A small script runs on those sites to put the covers back, and reads a page only to find what to cover.
