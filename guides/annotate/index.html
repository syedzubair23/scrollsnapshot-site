Make the important part obvious

Annotation turns a capture into an explanation. Draw a box around a faulty control, point an arrow at the exact state, or put a brief label where a reader will look first.

01

Marks stay aligned with the image

ScrollSnapshot stores mark positions in the image's own pixels. A tall screenshot may be displayed small on screen, but a box or arrow is mapped back to the full image before export.

02

Build a readable note

  1. Open Edit and choose a tool: Box, Arrow, Highlight, Text, or Pen.
  2. Use one colour and a small number of marks to establish the reading order.
  3. Apply the marks, then review the final file at the size your recipient will see.
KEEP IN MIND

Redactions are painted before other marks, so a later arrow cannot uncover information you covered.
