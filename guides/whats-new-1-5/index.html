What's new in ScrollSnapshot 1.5

Version 1.5 saves the frame a video is showing at the video's own resolution, with a burst of five around the moment, subtitles if you want them, and folders per platform.

IN THIS GUIDEVideo framesBurst, subtitles and foldersA capture button on videosA new shortcutGetting the updateAll guides ↗
01

Video frames

Choose Video in the popup, or press Alt+Shift+S, to save the frame a video is showing as a PNG at its own size. A copy-protected video is refused with a message, never saved as a black image.

02

Burst, subtitles and folders

Burst takes five frames around the moment and puts the video back as it was; the result page lets you pick one or save all five. Include subtitles puts the captions into the frame. Frames file into a folder per platform, on disk and in History.

03

A capture button on videos

Off until you turn it on. Point at a video and press the camera to save its frame; its menu downloads straight into the platform folder, sets burst and subtitles for one site, or hides the button there.

04

A new shortcut

Alt+Shift+S captures a video frame on new installs, and Slices no longer has a default shortcut. Existing installs keep Alt+Shift+S on Slices until they change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

05

Getting the update

  1. Chrome updates extensions on its own, usually within a day or two of a release.
  2. To check now, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, and choose Update.
  3. Look for version 1.5.0 on ScrollSnapshot's card.
