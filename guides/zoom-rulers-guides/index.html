Zoom in and measure

A long capture is hard to judge at fit size. Zoom in to check an edge, and use rulers, a grid, and guides to line things up. None of the measuring tools is ever saved into the image.

IN THIS GUIDEZoom and panRulers and the gridGuidesAll guides ↗
01

Zoom and pan

The result page, the crop box, and the editor all zoom, and they zoom the same way. Use the minus and plus buttons, hold Ctrl or Command and turn the mouse wheel, or press Ctrl or Command with 0 and 1. The readout between the buttons shows the current zoom, and pressing it toggles between fit and actual size. Hold Space and drag to pan once you are zoomed in.

  • Zoom to actual size to judge text sharpness. Fit size hides the detail you care about.
  • In the editor, zoom first and draw second. A mark placed at 200 percent lands more precisely.
02

Rulers and the grid

The Rulers and grid button on the toolbar opens two switches, Rulers and Grid. Rulers appear along the top and left of the capture, in image pixels. The grid lays regular lines over it, which helps you judge alignment and spacing.

03

Guides

Drag a guide out of a ruler to mark a position. Pull one from the top ruler for a horizontal line, or from the left ruler for a vertical one. Drag it back into the ruler to remove it. A tag shows the guide's position as you drag.

Guides, the grid, and the rulers are a view aid only. They are never saved into the image.

  1. Turn on Rulers and Grid from the toolbar button.
  2. Drag a guide from a ruler onto the edge you want to compare against.
  3. Zoom in and pan along it to check the alignment.
KEEP IN MIND

Guides and the grid exist only while you are looking at the capture. What you download is the capture, with your marks, and nothing else.
