Zoom in and measure

A long capture is hard to judge at fit size. Zoom in to check an edge, and use rulers, a grid, and guides to line things up. None of the measuring tools is saved into the image.

IN THIS GUIDEZoom and panRulers, grid, and guidesCheck an edgeAll guides ↗
01

Zoom and pan

The result page, the crop box, and the editor all zoom. Use the minus and plus buttons, press Ctrl or Command with the mouse wheel, or press Ctrl or Command with 0 and 1. The readout toggles between fit and actual size. Hold Space and drag to pan.

02

Rulers, grid, and guides

Turn on Rulers and grid on the toolbar. Drag a guide out of a ruler to mark a position, and drag it back to remove it. They are there to help you see: they are a view aid, never saved into the image.

03

Check an edge

  1. Zoom to actual size where the detail matters.
  2. Drag a guide from a ruler to the edge you want to compare against.
  3. Hold Space and drag to pan along it.
KEEP IN MIND

Guides and the grid exist only while you are looking at the capture. What you download is the capture, with your marks, and nothing else.
