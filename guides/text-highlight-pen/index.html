Style the text, highlight, and pen

The Text, Highlight, and Pen tools each have a panel of options. They set the next mark you draw and restyle a mark you have selected, and saved style presets remember them.

IN THIS GUIDETextHighlightPenMake it repeatableAll guides ↗
01

Text

The Text tool's size button opens a panel with size, font (sans, serif, mono, or hand), weight, and alignment. Italic, underline, background chip, and outline are switches. A background chip keeps small text readable over a busy page; an outline does the same on an image.

02

Highlight

Highlight's dropdown sets its strength from 25 to 80 percent and whether it is a block, an underline, or a strikethrough. A Multiply blend keeps dark text dark under a bright highlight, as a real marker would.

03

Pen

Pen's dropdown sets a solid, dashed, or dotted line, its strength, round or square ends, and smoothing for a steadier hand-drawn stroke.

04

Make it repeatable

  1. Choose a tool and set its options in the panel.
  2. Draw the mark, or select an existing one to restyle it.
  3. Save the look as a style preset if you will use it again.
KEEP IN MIND

Older text, highlights and strokes look exactly as they did. These options change only what you set from now on.
