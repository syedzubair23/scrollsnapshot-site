A burst of five frames

Fast motion rarely stops where you want it. With Burst on, one capture takes five frames around the moment so you can choose the sharp one.

IN THIS GUIDETurn it onWhat happens to the videoOn the result pageAll guides ↗
01

Turn it on

Switch on Burst of five frames in the popup's Video mode. Choose how far apart the frames are under Settings → Capture, in Time between burst frames: ⅛, ¼, ½ or 1 second. ¼ second is the default.

02

What happens to the video

ScrollSnapshot pauses and steps the video to each frame, muted, then puts it back exactly as it was: the same time, paused or playing, muted or not. A live stream can't go forward in time, so its later frames are collected by letting it play on.

03

On the result page

  1. Pick a frame in the strip above the image, or step through them with the arrow keys or the arrows at the sides.
  2. Crop or edit the frame you picked. Each frame keeps its own edits.
  3. Download, or Save all, saves all five frames, each as it currently is, as numbered files.
KEEP IN MIND

With Include subtitles also on, a site that draws its own subtitles (YouTube, for one) gives one frame, not five: the frame is taken from the screen so the subtitles show exactly as the site draws them. Turn subtitles off for a burst there.
