ScrollSnapshot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

In short: ScrollSnapshot captures screenshots of the page you tell it to, entirely on your own device. Nothing you capture — and no browsing data — is ever sent to us, to any server, or to any third party. There is no account, no sign-in, and no tracking or analytics of any kind.

What ScrollSnapshot does

ScrollSnapshot is a Chrome extension that captures the tab you are viewing: the visible area, the whole scrollable page, the page split into numbered image slices, or a region you draw or specify. It can also extract the text and colours shown in what you capture, and let you crop, redact or annotate the result before you save it.

Every one of these actions happens only when you explicitly trigger it — by clicking the extension's icon, using a keyboard shortcut, or clicking a button inside the extension's own popup or result page. ScrollSnapshot never captures, reads or acts on a page in the background or without that action from you.

What data ScrollSnapshot accesses, and why

Chrome requires an extension to declare the permissions it uses. Here is what each one is for and nothing more:

PermissionWhat it's used for
activeTab Lets the extension act on the tab you're currently looking at, only after you click its icon or a shortcut — the standard, narrowest way Chrome lets an extension read a page on request.
scripting Injects the small script that measures the page, scrolls it during a full-page capture, and reads the pixels, text or line positions you asked to capture — running only in the tab you triggered a capture in.
storage Saves your settings (default format, capture engine, etc.) and, if you enable capture history, your past captures — all inside your browser's own local storage. See "Where your data is stored" below.
downloads Lets you save a finished capture to disk when you click Download.
clipboardWrite Lets you copy a finished capture to your clipboard when you click Copy.
debugger Used only if you turn on the optional "Turbo" capture engine, which uses Chrome's own DevTools protocol to capture a page more precisely. Chrome shows a banner across your browser windows for as long as this engine is attached, and dismissing that banner cancels the capture in progress — that is a restriction Chrome itself imposes on any extension using this permission, not something ScrollSnapshot adds.
<all_urls> (host access) Needed so a capture triggered by a keyboard shortcut — where there is no click on the extension's icon to grant one-off access — can still run on whatever page you're viewing. ScrollSnapshot does not read, monitor or transmit anything from any page except at the moment you trigger a capture on it.

Where your data is stored

Everything ScrollSnapshot produces or remembers is stored locally, inside your own browser profile, using Chrome's built-in storage — the same mechanism any website uses to remember your preferences. Specifically:

  • Captures (if you turn on capture history) are kept in your browser's IndexedDB, on your device only, up to a limit you choose in Settings. You can clear them, or delete individual captures, at any time from the History page.
  • Settings (format, engine, quality, etc.) are kept in Chrome's storage API, which may sync across your own signed-in Chrome browsers the same way Chrome syncs bookmarks — that sync is Chrome's, not ScrollSnapshot's, and no ScrollSnapshot server is ever involved.

ScrollSnapshot has no server. It makes no network requests of its own, sends nothing it captures anywhere, and has no way to see what you have captured, stored, or deleted. There is nothing for us to collect, because nothing leaves your device.

Accounts, analytics and third parties

ScrollSnapshot has no account system, no sign-in, and collects no analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics. It does not use any third-party service, SDK, or advertising network of any kind.

Children's privacy

ScrollSnapshot is a general-purpose developer and productivity tool, not directed at children, and knowingly collects no data from anyone — children included, since it collects no data from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If ScrollSnapshot's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change with it. Since the extension has no way to contact you directly, please check back here if you want to confirm nothing has changed.
